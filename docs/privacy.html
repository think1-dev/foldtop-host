Privacy policy

Last updated 16 November 2026

Foldtop collects no data. The app talks only to the workstations you pair with it, and nothing is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

This policy covers the Foldtop app for iPhone and the Foldtop companion that runs on a workstation. "We" means the developer of Foldtop, named as the seller on the app's App Store page.

What we collect

Nothing. Foldtop has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We run no servers that the app or the companion contact. We cannot see your screen, your typing, your sound, your clipboard or which workstations you use.

What stays on your devices

  • On the iPhone: your settings, and for each workstation you pair its name, network addresses, certificate fingerprint and a key for that pairing. The key is kept in the iOS Keychain. Removing a workstation in the app, or deleting the app, removes them.
  • On the workstation: the companion's certificate and key, the list of paired devices (name, identifier and key) and optional settings, in the folder ~/.config/foldtop. The companion writes ordinary log lines, such as when a device connects and from which address, to the system journal on that workstation.

What travels between your iPhone and your workstation

While you are connected, the workstation sends its screen picture, pointer shape and sound to the iPhone, and the iPhone sends your touches and keystrokes to the workstation. If you switch them on, the iPhone's microphone audio and copied text are sent as well. All of it goes directly between your two devices over an encrypted connection, across your own network or VPN. It is not recorded by Foldtop and does not pass through any service of ours.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Local network: to find and connect to workstations on your network.
  • Camera: only to scan the pairing QR code shown by your workstation. Pictures are read on the iPhone and are not saved or sent.
  • Microphone: only while you have Microphone switched on in a session. The audio is sent to the workstation you are connected to and nowhere else.
  • Pasteboard: when Share Clipboard is on, text you copied on the iPhone is sent to the workstation you are connected to, and text copied on the workstation is placed on the iPhone's clipboard. You can turn this off in the app's settings.

Apple

The app is distributed by Apple. If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may give us anonymous, aggregated statistics such as crash reports and download counts. That is governed by Apple's own privacy policy, and it does not include anything from inside your sessions.

Support requests

If you open an issue on our GitHub issues page, what you write there is public and is handled by GitHub under its own privacy policy. Do not include passwords, pairing codes or anything else private.

Children

Foldtop is a utility for people who use a workstation. It is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date at the top.

Contact
